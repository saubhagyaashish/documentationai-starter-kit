## What Is TradeBotX?

TradeBotX is a full-stack trading intelligence platform built for the **Indian stock market (NSE)**. It combines a 13-agent AI pipeline powered by LLMs with real-time market data from Upstox, automated technical analysis, and a simulated paper trading engine — all accessible through a modern React dashboard.

Think of it as your personal AI trading desk: it screens stocks, debates bull vs. bear cases using multiple AI agents, computes technical signals, and can auto-trade in paper mode — without risking real money.

<Callout kind="info">
  TradeBotX is designed for **paper trading only**. No real orders are placed on any exchange. It is a research and strategy-testing tool.
</Callout>

## Why TradeBotX?

| Problem | TradeBotX Solution |
|---|---|
| Manual stock research takes hours | 13 AI agents analyse a stock in ~2 minutes |
| Emotional trading decisions | Data-driven composite signal scores (0.0–1.0) |
| Missing market moves | Auto-scanner monitors NIFTY 50 every 5 minutes |
| No way to test strategies | Paper trading with full risk management |
| Expensive trading terminals | Free, self-hosted, open-source |
| Information overload | Curated analysis: macro, fundamentals, sentiment, technicals |

## The React Dashboard

The frontend has six tabs, each serving a distinct purpose:

| Tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Screener** | NSE index stock screener with flagged stocks |
| **Analysis** | Live agent pipeline progress and final results |
| **Paper Trading** | Live prices, signal table, portfolio, trade history |
| **Watchlist** | Custom ticker list and batch analysis launcher |
| **Predictions** | AI decision accuracy tracking dashboard |
| **Results** | Historical analysis results browser |

## Overview

The `technical.py` module computes all technical indicators **locally** using pandas and numpy. No external TA library (TA-Lib, pandas-ta, etc.) is required. The module takes raw OHLCV candle data from Upstox and returns a composite signal score between **0.0** and **1.0**.

## Dual-Timeframe Analysis

The engine uses two timeframes simultaneously to capture both trend and intraday momentum:

| Timeframe | Data Source | Indicators Computed |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily** (100 days) | Upstox historical candles | RSI, MACD, EMA-20/50, Bollinger Bands, ATR |
| **Intraday 5-min** (today) | Upstox intraday candles | VWAP, Intraday Momentum |

<Callout kind="info">
  When intraday data is unavailable (market closed, weekends, or public holidays), the engine substitutes **SMA-200** as a proxy for VWAP, ensuring the score remains meaningful outside market hours.
</Callout>

## Indicators and Weights

| Indicator | Weight | What It Measures |
|---|---|---|
| **MACD** (12 / 26 / 9) | 20% | Trend direction and crossover signals |
| **EMA-20 & EMA-50** | 15% | Short and medium-term trend confirmation |
| **VWAP** (5-min intraday) | 15% | Fair price relative to volume-weighted average |
| **Volume Surge** (2× 20-day avg) | 15% | Institutional/unusual buying or selling activity |
| **RSI** (14-period) | 9% | Overbought / oversold momentum |
| **Bollinger Bands** (20 / 2σ) | 10% | Volatility and mean-reversion positioning |
| **Intraday Momentum** | 10% | Real-time candle direction (last 5-min candles) |
| **Stochastic %K** | 6% | Price position within its recent high-low range |

Total weight sums to **100%**.

## How Each Indicator Is Scored

Each indicator produces a sub-score between 0.0 and 1.0 before being weighted:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="RSI">
    | RSI Value | Sub-score | Interpretation |
    |---|---|---|
    | < 30 | 0.9 | Strongly oversold → bullish |
    | 30 – 40 | 0.7 | Mildly oversold |
    | 40 – 60 | 0.5 | Neutral |
    | 60 – 70 | 0.3 | Mildly overbought |
    | > 70 | 0.1 | Strongly overbought → bearish |
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="MACD">
    Scored on two signals combined:
    1. **Histogram sign** — positive histogram → bullish (0.7), negative → bearish (0.3)
    2. **Crossover recency** — a recent bullish crossover (MACD line crossing above signal) within the last 3 bars adds +0.2 to the sub-score; a bearish crossover subtracts 0.2.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="VWAP">
    | Price vs. VWAP | Sub-score |
    |---|---|
    | Price > VWAP by > 0.5% | 0.8 — strong bullish positioning |
    | Price ≈ VWAP (±0.5%) | 0.5 — neutral |
    | Price < VWAP by > 0.5% | 0.2 — bearish positioning |
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Bollinger Bands">
    | Price Position | Sub-score |
    |---|---|
    | Near lower band (< 20th percentile of band width) | 0.8 — mean-reversion buy signal |
    | Middle zone | 0.5 — neutral |
    | Near upper band (> 80th percentile) | 0.2 — mean-reversion sell signal |
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Volume Surge">
    | Volume vs. 20-day Average | Sub-score |
    |---|---|
    | > 2× average (surge) with up day | 0.9 — institutional buying |
    | > 2× average (surge) with down day | 0.1 — institutional selling |
    | 1–2× average | 0.5 — normal |
    | < 1× average | 0.4 — low participation |
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Composite Signal Score

The final score is a weighted sum:

```
Score = (RSI × 0.09) + (Stochastic × 0.06) + (MACD × 0.20) +
        (EMA × 0.15) + (VWAP × 0.15) + (Bollinger × 0.10) +
        (VolumeSurge × 0.15) + (IntradayMomentum × 0.10)
```

## Signal Interpretation

| Score Range | Signal | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 0.65 | **BUY** | Strong bullish confluence across most indicators |
| 0.35 – 0.64 | **HOLD** | Mixed or conflicting signals — no edge |
| ≤ 0.35 | **SELL** | Strong bearish confluence across most indicators |

<Callout kind="tip">
  The auto-scanner uses these same thresholds to trigger paper trades automatically: scores ≥ 0.65 on an upward-moving stock trigger a BUY; scores ≤ 0.35 on a held position trigger an exit.
</Callout>

## Additional Metrics

Besides the composite score, the engine also computes and returns:

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| **ATR** (14-period) | Average True Range — used to set stop-loss distances |
| **EMA-200** | Long-term trend filter (used as VWAP fallback) |
| **Support / Resistance** | Simple swing-high / swing-low detection over 20-day window |
| **Trend Direction** | `"uptrend"`, `"downtrend"`, or `"sideways"` based on EMA alignment |
